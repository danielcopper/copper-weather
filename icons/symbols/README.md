# Material Symbols

Copies of Google's Material Symbols, the Rounded style at its defaults (weight 400, no fill, grade 0, 24 px), from
`google/material-design-icons` at commit `10ecc14`, each unchanged and under its Material Symbols name. They are
licensed under the Apache License 2.0, in [LICENSE](LICENSE) beside them.

`scripts/warning_symbols.py` makes the warnings' symbols of them (D48): most as they are; frost, heat and ice changed,
each composed of two of them, the second scaled to 0.55 and placed at the upper right of the first; and UV changed, the
rays of `sunny` without its disc and the letters "UV" in their middle, which the script draws itself and which fall
under the repository's licence. Each generated drawable names its sources and what it changes in a comment at its top.
